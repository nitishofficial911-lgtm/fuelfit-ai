About this version

FuelFit AI is a front-end prototype. Entries are stored in this browser only. There is no account system, cloud sync, automatic AI food recognition or live nutrition database connected yet. The scanner currently uses a user-confirmed food match and typical per-100 g estimates.

Before a public launch, add secure authentication, privacy controls, data deletion, a backend and clear nutrition limitations. Do not present AI estimates as medical advice.
